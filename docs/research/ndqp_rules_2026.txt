2026 ND QSO Party Rules

North Dakota QSO Party Sponsored by: ARRL ND Section Manager N0RDF - and
ASM K0YL

Date/Time: Starts at 1800Z (1:00 PM CDST) April 11th, 2026 until 1800Z (1:00 PM
CDST) April 12th, 2026 Bands: 160 through 10 meters, 6 & 2 meters, (Excluding
the WARC Bands)

Use of a Repeater is not allowed.

Objective:

ND stations to contact as many counties, states, provinces as possible. All Outside
North Dakota stations to contact as many ND stations as possible. A station can
be fixed, portable or mobile. They will count as one station.

Exchange: ND stations give RST and County. U.S. and Canadian stations give RST
and State, Province or Territories DX Stations give RST and DX country.

Modes: Phone= (SSB/FM both counts as Phone); CW= (CW counts as CW) and
Digital = (RTTY/PSK), NO FT8,

Remotes allowed only through your home station.

Scoring: All contacts count as 1 point per non-duplicated phone, CW or Digital
contact on each band.

Multipliers: ND stations multiply points by the sum of ND Counties (53),
states/provinces (49 states excluding North Dakota + DC, + 10 Canadian provinces
+ 3 Canadian Territories = 63 W/VE multipliers total): 116 Total Multipliers for
W/VE (Outside North Dakota stations) and DX stations are the 53 ND counties.

You are allowed to make a Phone, CW and a Digital contact with the same station
on the same band in each mode.

North Dakota stations may work DXCC countries for points only - no multipliers.
Multipliers count once overall, not once per band or mode.
Awards Overall Champion Certificates will be awarded to the Highest scoring ND
Station, (Either Fixed, Portable or Mobile)

Highest Scoring Outside ND (US) Station, Highest Scoring Canadian or DX Station.

Second Place Honor Roll Certificates Second highest-scoring ND Station, (Either
Fixed, Portable or Mobile)

Highest Scoring Outside DX Station

Highest Phone Score

Highest CW Score

Highest Digital Score

There will also be a beautiful Plaque from the ND Section Manager, as well as a
Handmade inlayed call sign which can be displayed on a desk, donated by ND0B,
for the highest scoring ND individual station.

ND QSO Party Rules: No Remote or Internet QSO’s. Stations may operate only
one transmitter at a time using the same call sign.

Club entries are welcome and appreciated. CW, Digital or Phone contacts will all
count one point.

Stations may be worked once per band and mode, and once per
band/mode/county for ND stations.

Each mobile vehicle is considered one station and must use only one call.

Mobile stations may park on a county line but each county must be worked in a
separate contact. There are no power limitations in this contest, within legal limit.

Mobile ND stations that change counties are considered to be a new station in
each new county. List of ND Counties with 3 letter abbreviations are included at
the end of this document.

All operators are asked to use these abbreviations in the exchange.
Standard State and Country abbreviations should be used.

All Canadian Provinces & Territories count as multipliers for ND stations.

There are no multi-op categories therefore, a club station cannot count for overall
Champion; mobile operators are encouraged to drive with a friend for safety.

Self spotting is not allowed.

For Paper Logs: An official ND QSO Party summary sheet must accompany your
log. ALL entries without a summary sheet will be considered to be check log and
won't be included in the final results.

 Logs shall indicate UTC, the call of the station worked, the band and mode, RST
sent and received, and the state, province, ND county, or DX country. ND mobiles
must indicate county changes in the log.

For handwritten logs: Please circle new multipliers as worked. Include $2.00 to
handle postage & supplies if you wish to receive the printed results by mail.

If you become a ND QSO Party winner and want the certificate mailed to you,
please send $2.00. Entries must be postmarked by May 15th, 2025.

 Entries may be disqualified if the rules of the North Dakota QSO Party are not
followed. The decision of the North Dakota QSO Party Committee shall be final.
