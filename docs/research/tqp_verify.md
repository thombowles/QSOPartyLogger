## Dates (2026) — CONFIRMED
DATES page: "2026 CONTEST DATES Saturday September 19 ... Saturday September 19: 1400Z to SEP 20 0200Z and Sunday September 20: 1400Z to 2000Z". Matches the State QSO Party Challenge calendar values given.

## Bands — CONFIRMED
RULES: "Operation on all Amateur bands is permitted, except on 60 meters, 30 meters, 17 meters and 12 meters."
So 160m IS in; VHF/UHF IS in (suggested freqs: "VHF—50.200 MHz and 144.200 MHz"); WARC 30/17/12 excluded, plus 60m.

## Points by mode — CONFIRMED (both TX and non-TX stations)
RULES: "Count two (2) points per phone QSO with any station." / "Count three (3) points per CW and other digital mode QSOs with any station." (Non-TX section: same 2/3, "with any Texas station".)
Digital includes RTTY/JT65/FT8: "For duping purposes, all digital sub modes are considered to be the same mode (Digital). So, working a fixed given station on RTTY and again on JT65 or FT8 on the same band will result in credit for one QSO."
New 2026 note: "Digital modes must be capable of sending and receiving the full contest exchange. A special fork of WSJT-X that supports this for FT-4/8 will be released prior to the 2026 TQP."

## Dupe rule — CONFIRMED
RULES: "Stations may be worked only once per band per mode for QSO count." / "Texas mobiles may be worked only once per band per mode from each county for QSO count."

## Multipliers — CONFIRMED
TX stations: "the number of states worked (not including Texas) plus the number of Texas counties worked plus each Canadian province worked (13 total) plus each DXCC country worked excluding USA, Canada, Alaska and Hawaii." (states max = 49; also "Washington DC QSOs count as Maryland contacts.")
Non-TX: "Your multiplier is the number of Texas counties worked. The maximum possible multiplier is 254."
Counted once: "Multipliers are counted once for the QSO Party. For example, if you work NC on SSB and on CW that counts as one multiplier."

## Mobile bonus (worked-mobile bonus, TX and non-TX chasers) — CONFIRMED
RULES: "Add five hundred (500) bonus points to your FINAL SCORE for each Texas mobile worked in five (5) different counties regardless of band or mode. If you work the same Texas mobile in five (5) additional counties, you add an additional five hundred (500) bonus points ... However, for bonus points you can only count one (1) contact per county per mobile."

## Other bonuses — CONFIRMED (none)
No bonus stations, no 1x1 bonus anywhere on the rules page. Only the 500-pt mobile bonus (and garbled TX-mobile 1000-pt clause) exist.

## County-line — CONFIRMED (no stated max)
RULES: "If a Texas mobile and/or a portable station is on a county line or junction between 2 or more Texas counties, a separate QSO and exchange must be logged for each county worked (e.g. SSB: NM5G 59 HARR, 59 MGMY)." No numeric cap stated; station "must have visual contact with a county line marker."
