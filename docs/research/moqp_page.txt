What is the Missouri QSO Party?

The Missouri QSO Party is open for participation by all licensed amateur operators
world-wide. The object of the contest is to work as many stations as possible during
the contest period, to provide all licensed radio amateurs outside the state of Missouri
the opportunity to work Missouri counties, and to provide all licensed radio amateurs
in Missouri an opportunity to work other states and countries.
