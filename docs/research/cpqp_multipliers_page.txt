The following chart shows abbreviations sent by VA/VE4, 5 and 6 stations*:
* Stations within the Canadian prairie provinces who work each other will log 3-letter abbreviations. They will receive a provincial multiplier for the first VA/VE4, 5 and 6 station worked on each band, including their own province. This will make a total of 63 multipliers for stations within the Canadian prairie provinces.
