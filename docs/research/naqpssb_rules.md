# North American QSO Party, SSB — official rules research

Researched 2026-07-27 for the 2026 runnings (January already past; August
15–16 upcoming). Template per constitution Article 15. One NCJ document
governs all three NAQP modes, so everything structural is shared with
[`naqpcw_rules.md`](naqpcw_rules.md); this file is self-contained on every
load-bearing fact and records what differs for SSB.

**Not a State QSO Party Challenge contest** — same requirement as the CW
party: a logged NAQP SSB shows under "not approved" and adds nothing to the
Challenge score (KE5CW, 2026-07-27).

## 2. Dates and times for 2026, UTC

Rule 4, quoted (SSB rows):

> "SSB  1800 UTC Jan 17 to 0559 UTC Jan 18  0600 UTC January 25  3rd full weekend January
>       1800 UTC Aug 15 to 0559 UTC Aug 16  0600 UTC August 23   3rd full weekend August"

With rules 5A(v)/5B(v)'s exact endpoint ("The contest period ends at
05:59:59 UTC") and the repo's exclusive-end encoding:

- **2026-01-17 18:00:00Z → 2026-01-18 06:00:00Z** (already run)
- **2026-08-15 18:00:00Z → 2026-08-16 06:00:00Z**

Formula cross-check: Jan 17 and Aug 15 are both the Saturdays of their
months' third full weekends in 2026. Printed dates and formulas agree.
Note the August SSB weekend collides with the Hawaii QSO Party's Aug 22
window only by proximity, not overlap — no interaction.

## 3. Exchange

Rule 10 — identical to CW: operator name and station location (state,
province, or country) for North American stations; name only for non-NA
stations, entered as `DX`. One name for the whole contest. No RST, no
serial. (`exchangeIncludesRST: false`, `exchangeIncludesName: true`.)

## 4. QSO points by mode

One point per valid contact (rule 13), and rule 7 permits exactly one mode:
"SSB only in phone parties." Points table ships 1/1/1 with
`allowedModes: ["phone"]` — a CW or digital row in this log is invalid.

## 5. Dupe rule

Rule 12: once per band ("Stations may be worked once per band"), which the
`bandMode` scope reproduces exactly under a single allowed mode.

## 6. Multipliers

Rule 11, identical to CW and quoted in full in `naqpcw_rules.md` §6: 50 US
states + DC (its own multiplier, never aliased), the standard 13 Canadian
provinces/territories (`NL` spelling), and the checklist's 47 other NA
entities, all counted **once per band** ("Multipliers count again on each
band"), on both sides identically — the party has no home state and no
asymmetry. Non-NA stations, maritime mobiles, and aeronautical mobiles are
QSO credit only, entered as `DX`, never a multiplier. The entrant's own
state counts like any other.

## 7. Bonus stations and bonus points

**NONE.**

## 8. Final-score multipliers

**NONE.** Power classes (QRP / Low / >100 W check log) are categories only.

## 9. County-line / multi-county rules

Not a county party; one location per exchange (`maxSimultaneousCounties: 1`).

## 10. Valid bands

Rule 8: 160/80/40/20/15/10 m (the RTTY 160 m exclusion does not apply to
SSB). Six bands, no WARC, no VHF.

## 11. Categories

Rule 5/6: SO, SOA, M2; QRP and Low power (over 100 W = check log). Team
competition is registration-side only. M2's 10-minute band timer is not
modeled (see CW §14).

## 12. Cabrillo `CONTEST:` header

Rules print no token; WA7BNM registry
(<https://www.contestcalendar.com/cabnames.php>, read 2026-07-27) is the
Article 1 authority: entry **[229] "North American QSO Party, SSB" →
`NAQP-SSB`**. No alias listed.

## 13. County list

The identical 46-of-47 entity list generated by `gen_naqp.py` for both NAQP
parties from the sponsor's checklist + the ARRL DXCC List — including the HI
(Dominican Republic) omission behind the Hawaii state token, `4U1` standing
in for the checklist's untypeable "4U1/u", and Cuba as `CM`. Full analysis
in `naqpcw_rules.md` §13; the generator asserts both parties' lists are
byte-identical.

## 14. Engine shapes to watch

Identical to `naqpcw_rules.md` §14 — countries-as-counties (BCQP precedent),
the no-mult `DX` token (NDQP shape), the name exchange this party co-forced
into existence, every entrant out-of-state to the engine, the deliberate
non-modeling of off-time/M2/penalty arithmetic, and two windows six months
apart. The only SSB-specific difference in the definition is
`allowedModes: ["phone"]`, the schedule, the Cabrillo name, and the id.
