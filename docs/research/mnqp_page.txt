28th Annual Minnesota QSO Party

Presented by the Minnesota Wireless Association

Saturday, February 7th, 2026

Hours: 1400 UTC (8:00 AM CST) to 2400 UTC (6:00 PM CST)

MN QSO Party Rules

County multiplier list with abbreviations

Upload your log here. Logs are due within 7 days of the contest end.

Plaques and certificates for winners in all categories.

Special gift of Minnesota wild rice for Top 10 non-MN scores.

Send your operation plans to webmaster@w0aa.org to be included on the Activity Map and Mobile Operations pages.

Spotting

Many of the mobile stations will be using APRS. You can track them with links on the Activity Map or Mobile Operations page.

You can track ALL APRS equipped mobile stations at one time on https://mobiletracker.stateqso.com/MN/.

We encourage the use of spotting networks. In addition to the normal DX Cluster sites you may want to try the QSOPartyHub site, the County Hunting Spots site, and the Reverse Beacon Network.

Real Time Contesting

The MN QSO Party is supported by the Real Time Contesting Scoreboard to see your score in real time. Go to hamscore.com to view the scoreboard. Instructions on setting up your logger to participate in Real Time Scoring ar at blog.contestonlinescore.com.
