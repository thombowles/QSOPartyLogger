RESULT: all three sponsor lists agree on the same 85 abbreviations. Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut are not separate sections: the sponsor says they 'should continue to send TER (Territories)'. The list contains no line named Hawaii; it contains 'Pacific  PAC' in the U.S. Call Area 6 group (the pages fetched do not define which places PAC covers — NOT STATED there). Alaska is 'AK' (Call Area 7 group); Puerto Rico 'PR' and US Virgin Islands 'VI' (Call Area 4 group). There is no 'MAR' (Maritime) and no 'GTA' in the current list (ARRL news 2023-11-17: 'RAC replaced Maritime (MAR) by making New Brunswick (NB) and Nova Scotia (NS) individual Sections, Greater Toronto Area (GTA) was renamed Golden Horseshoe (GH), and Northern Territories was renamed as Territories (TER)').
